## About This Compendium

This compendium gathers the complete worldbuilding of the fantasy world **Mounet** — covering geography, civilization, races, magic, faith, nations, society, monsters, and adventurers — for readers and creators alike.

This is a public setting reference and contains **no story spoilers**.

## World at a Glance

The world of **Mounet** consists of three main continents, linked by land bridges and sea routes:

- **Terra (the Main Continent)** — at the center, homeland of the elves, now held solely by the Oleg Empire.
- **Nor (the Northern Continent)** — to the north, homeland of humanity, guarded by the Western Six Nations.
- **Suda (the Demon Continent)** — to the southwest, homeland of demons and dwarves, home to three nations from north to south.
- **The Southern Seas** — a long, slender island: the pureblood elf kingdom of Ipre Galia.
